## Days 1-7: Showing Up
Build the habit and learn to notice what's happening in your body and mind.

### Day 1: Just Breathe
Sit comfortably and close your eyes. Feel the natural sensation of the breath in the nose
or the belly, without controlling it. When the mind wanders, gently come back.
- Breath awareness | 5m

### Day 2: Body Scan
Move your attention slowly from head to toes. Don't change anything, just notice.
- Body scan | 5m

### Day 3: Sound as Anchor
Rest your attention on sounds, near and far, and the silence between them.
- Listening | 5m

### Day 4: Counting Breaths
Count each exhale from one to ten, then start again. Lost count? Begin at one.
- Breath counting | 5m

### Day 5: Breath and Body
- Breath awareness | 3m
- Body scan | 2m

### Day 6: Three Anchors
Rest on the breath, the weight of your body, and the sounds around you. Return to any one.
- Open anchors | 5m

### Day 7: Reflection
Sit quietly and ask: what was hardest this week, and what surprised me?
- Reflection | 5m | note: no timer needed

## Days 8-14: Deepening Attention
Lengthen the sessions and work with distraction skilfully.

### Day 8: Longer Breath
Expect restlessness around minute five. That's normal. Keep returning.
- Breath awareness | 10m

### Day 9: Naming Thoughts
When a thought arises, label it quietly: thinking, planning, worrying. Then return.
- Noting | 10m

### Day 10: Full Body Scan
- Body scan | 10m | note: extra time on jaw, shoulders and belly

### Day 11: Breath in the Belly
- Belly breathing | 10m

### Day 12: Open Awareness
Let go of a single anchor and rest in whatever is most noticeable.
- Open awareness | 10m

### Day 13: Loving-Kindness
After a few minutes on the breath, repeat: may I be safe, happy, healthy, at ease.
- Loving-kindness | 10m

### Day 14: Reflection
- Reflection | 5m | note: journal if it helps

## Days 15-21: Integrating
Extend the practice and start carrying it into the day.

### Day 15: The Full Sit
Set the timer for fifteen minutes and stay until it rings, even if you're restless.
- Breath awareness | 15m

### Day 16: Mindful Movement
- Body scan | 5m
- Neck and shoulder rolls | 5m
- Breath awareness | 5m

### Day 17: Thoughts as Clouds
- Open awareness | 15m | note: you are the sky, not the clouds

### Day 18: Loving-Kindness Expanded
Yourself, someone you love, a stranger, then someone difficult. Same phrases for each.
- Loving-kindness | 15m

### Day 19: No Clock-Watching
- Open awareness | 15m | note: don't check the timer until it rings

### Day 20: Your Own Practice
Combine whatever has worked best for you: breath, body, sounds, kindness.
- Your practice | 15m

### Day 21: Looking Forward
Sit with open awareness, then reflect: what changed, and what do I want to keep?
- Open awareness | 15m
- Reflection | 5m
